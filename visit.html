Weaving days

The barn is heated. The porch is not. Pull into the gravel on the mill side. The blue door is us.

Monday through Wednesday we’re weaving. Knock if you’re picking up an order. Otherwise, wait for Thursday.

Leashed dogs are welcome. Baskets are not chew toys, though several have tried.
